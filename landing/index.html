How a change gets approved

  1. 1

    Write it up on site

    What’s changing, the price before tax, and the time it adds. You see the exact text before it goes.

  2. 2

    Send it from your own number

    Your phone’s Messages app opens with the text ready. Clients see your name, not a strange number.

  3. 3

    They tap Approve

    No app and no login. They type their name to confirm, and JobThread records who approved what, the price and the time.

  4. 4

    The total takes care of itself

    Approved changes add to the job total with HST. Anything still waiting is listed, not counted.

Nothing for your clients to download

Clients get a text from you with a link. It opens in their browser, shows the change or quote in plain numbers with tax, and asks them to type their name to approve. That’s it.

Records that don’t change after the fact

Once a change is sent, its price and description are locked. To fix a mistake you void it and send a new one, and both stay in the job’s history. Approvals keep the name, time and price.

Questions

Do my clients need an app or an account?

No. They tap the link in your text and it opens in their phone’s browser.

Does it text from my number?

Yes. JobThread writes the text and opens your Messages app; you send it yourself, so it comes from you.

What does an approval record?

The change number, description, price, the name the client typed, and the date and time. It can’t be edited afterwards. Talk to your lawyer about how you use approvals in your contracts.

Does it handle HST and holdbacks?

Yes. Prices go in before tax, HST is shown on everything the client sees, and a 10% holdback can be tracked per job.

Does it take payments?

Not yet. It sends payment requests with your e-Transfer details and keeps track of what’s been paid.

What does it cost?

It’s free during the pilot. We’ll give plenty of notice before that changes.
