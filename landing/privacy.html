Privacy Policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

JobThread (“we”, “us”) is run by [LEGAL BUSINESS NAME] in Ontario, Canada. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and what you can do about it. We follow Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

Who this covers

  • Contractors who sign up and use JobThread (“you”).
  • Your clients and trades, whose details you add, and who open the links you send them.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email, name, business name, mobile number, and optional details you add in Settings (e-Transfer email, Google review link, rating, credentials).
  • What you put in about jobs: client names, phone numbers, emails and addresses; job details, prices, changes, quotes, schedules, payments, notes, photos, site access details and lockbox codes; and the trades you work with.
  • What clients and trades do on a link: the name a client types to approve a change or accept a quote, the time they did it, and their browser type; when a job sheet or proposal is opened; and the name a trade types to see a lockbox code.
  • Technical basics: sign-in records and standard server logs, used to keep the service running and secure.

We don’t use advertising trackers, and we don’t sell personal information.

Why we use it

  • To run JobThread: sign you in, store your jobs, and show clients and trades the pages you send them.
  • To keep a reliable record of approvals, with names and times that can’t be edited later.
  • To contact you about your account, support, and changes to JobThread.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

Your clients’ information

You decide what client and trade information goes into JobThread, and you’re responsible for having their OK to use it for your work with them. We only use it to provide the service to you. Only you can see your jobs; a client or trade sees only the page in the link you send them.

Who we share it with

We use a few service providers to run JobThread. They only process information on our behalf:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and photo storage.
  • Netlify: hosting the website and app.
  • Resend: sending sign-in emails.

These providers store and process data in the United States, so your information may be subject to US law, including access by US authorities. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Texts are sent from your own phone’s Messages app, not by us, so your carrier handles them under its own terms.

How we protect it

Data is encrypted in transit. Each contractor’s data is kept separate by database access rules, links use long random codes, and lockbox codes stay hidden until a trade asks for them. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach puts you at real risk of significant harm, we’ll tell you and the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as the law requires.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. If you close your account, we delete your jobs and account information within 90 days, except where the law requires us to keep something longer.

Your choices

You can see and edit most of your information in the app. To get a copy of your information, correct it, or close your account, email hello@jobthread.ca. Clients and trades can contact the contractor who sent them the link, or us.

Questions or complaints

Email hello@jobthread.ca. If we can’t resolve your concern, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we’ll tell you by email or in the app before it takes effect.
